# Safety-first audit

Run these in order. Mark each **Pass / Gap / N/A**, size the gap in rupees, and give the fix. Do not move to tax or returns advice while a **Critical** item is a Gap — fixing it comes first in the action list (you may still describe tax/returns findings, but rank them below).

## A. Compliance safety (Critical)

Tax trouble — notices, penalties, prosecution — destroys more value than any optimisation saves.

1. **Filing status.** ITRs filed for recent years; none pending. Belated/updated return options if one was missed (verify the current windows and additional-tax rates).
2. **AIS reconciliation.** Every AIS/TIS line is either in the return or has a documented reason it isn't (e.g. duplicate reporting). Mismatches trigger automated notices.
3. **Foreign assets.** A resident (ordinarily resident) must report foreign assets held at any time in the year — including RSUs/ESPP shares, foreign bank accounts and cash left in a foreign brokerage — in the foreign assets schedule, even if no income arose. Non-disclosure carries steep penalties under the Black Money Act; verify the current penalty thresholds and whether a disclosure scheme is open.
4. **Advance tax.** If net tax after TDS is at or above the threshold, instalments are due quarterly. Missed instalments cost interest. Salaried people with capital gains, rent, foreign income or freelance income are the usual misses.
5. **TDS credit and TDS on payments the user makes** — e.g. TDS obligations when buying property above the threshold or paying high rent; often forgotten.
6. **High-risk claims.** HRA paid to family without a genuine arrangement, deductions without proof, cash transactions above reporting limits. Flag and either document properly or drop.
7. **Residency correctness.** Days-in-India count determines what is taxable. Moving abroad mid-year or returning needs a deliberate check.

## B. Liquidity (Critical)

8. **Emergency fund.** Default target: 6 months of essential expenses + EMIs; 9–12 months if income is volatile, single-earner, contract-based, or in a sector with layoffs. Held in instruments with same-day/next-day access (savings, sweep FD, liquid/overnight fund). Not in equity, not in locked products.
9. **Near-term goals (< 3 years)** funded from low-volatility instruments, not equity.

## C. Protection (Critical)

10. **Term life cover** if anyone depends on the user's income. Rough floor: enough to clear all debt plus 10–15× annual expenses of dependants, minus liquid assets. Personal policy, not only employer cover (employer cover ends with the job). No endowment/ULIP as a substitute.
11. **Health cover.** Personal family floater independent of employer, with a super top-up for size. Parents covered separately. Employer-only cover is a Gap.
12. **Disability / accident cover** for those with dependants and high income reliance (Important, not Critical).

## D. Debt (High)

13. **High-cost debt** (credit card revolve, personal loans, BNPL, consumer loans) cleared before investing beyond the emergency fund. Paying off 14–40% debt is a guaranteed risk-free return.
14. **Home loan**: prepay-vs-invest judged on post-tax loan rate vs. post-tax expected return *and* the user's comfort with leverage. Note that under the new regime there is no interest deduction on a self-occupied home, which raises the effective loan cost.

## E. Concentration and counterparty (High)

15. **Employer stock** above ~10% of net worth: plan a staged diversification, scheduling sales to use the annual LTCG exemption and holding periods.
16. **Single-bank deposits** above the DICGC insurance limit (₹5 lakh per depositor per bank, verify) — spread across banks or move excess to government securities / gilt or liquid funds.
17. **Unregulated products**: P2P lending, unlisted "assured return" schemes, crypto beyond a small risk-capital slice, private credit from non-regulated entities. Cap or exit.
18. **Single-property concentration** where real estate is most of net worth with a large loan.

## F. Estate (Important)

19. Nominations updated on every account, policy, demat and MF folio.
20. A registered or at least witnessed will if there are dependants or property.

## Output

A table: `# | Item | Status | Gap (₹) | Fix | Priority`. Then a one-line verdict: "Safe to optimise" or "Fix items X, Y first".
