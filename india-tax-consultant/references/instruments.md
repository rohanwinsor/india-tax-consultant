# Instruments: safety, tax, liquidity

Ratings are qualitative. Always compute **post-tax** return for the user's slab before comparing, and verify current rates (PPF/SSY/NSC rates reset quarterly; FD rates move with policy rates).

| Instrument | Safety (capital) | Tax treatment | Liquidity | Lock-in | Best use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Savings account / sweep FD | Very high (DICGC ₹5L/bank) | Interest at slab | Same day | None | Emergency fund (part) |
| Bank FD | Very high up to DICGC limit; bank risk above | Interest at slab, taxed yearly on accrual; TDS | Premature exit with penalty | Term | Short goals, emergency fund (part) |
| Overnight / liquid fund | High | Slab (specified MF) on redemption only | T+1 | None | Emergency fund, parking |
| Arbitrage fund | High (low volatility) | Equity taxation: 20% STCG, 12.5% LTCG | T+1/2 | None (exit load ~30 days) | Short-term parking for high-slab users |
| Gilt / G-sec / T-bills (RBI Retail Direct) | Sovereign | Interest at slab | Market-linked; T-bills short | Term | Safe debt, deposits above DICGC limit |
| Target-maturity / debt MFs | High–medium (credit, rate risk) | Slab if bought ≥ 1 Apr 2023 | T+1/2 | None | Medium-term debt allocation |
| PPF | Sovereign | Exempt on contribution (old regime), interest and maturity | Low | 15 years (partial from yr 7) | Long-term debt for anyone; best in old regime |
| EPF / VPF | Sovereign-backed | Interest tax-free up to the employee-contribution threshold; above it taxable | Low | Till job change/retirement rules | Core retirement debt |
| NPS Tier I | Market-linked, regulated | Employer contribution deductible (both regimes); lump sum mostly exempt at 60; annuity taxed | Very low | Till 60, partial annuity | Retirement, when employer NPS is offered |
| Sovereign Gold Bond | Sovereign + gold price risk | 2.5% interest at slab; maturity gains tax-free only if bought at issue | Low (exchange-traded, thin) | 8 years (exit from yr 5) | Gold allocation (no new issues recently — verify) |
| Gold ETF / gold MF | Gold price risk | LTCG 12.5% after 12 months (ETF); verify for FoFs | T+1 | None | Gold allocation 5–10% |
| Index funds / ETFs (Indian equity) | Market risk | 20% STCG / 12.5% LTCG above ₹1.25L | T+1/2 | None | Core long-term growth |
| Active equity funds / ELSS | Market risk | Same as equity; ELSS 80C in old regime | T+2 (ELSS 3-yr lock) | ELSS 3 yrs | Long-term growth |
| Direct stocks | High single-name risk | Equity taxation | T+1 | None | Only within a capped slice |
| Foreign equity (US via LRS, RSUs) | Market + currency risk | Slab if < 24 months; 12.5% LTCG after; foreign dividends at slab with credit for US withholding | T+1 to days | None | Diversification; watch TCS and reporting |
| International FoFs (Indian MF) | Market + currency | Verify — classification depends on equity share | T+3 | None | Simpler foreign exposure |
| REITs / InvITs | Medium | Distribution taxed by component; units as listed securities | T+1 | None | Small income slice |
| Real estate (physical) | Illiquid, concentration | Rent at slab (30% standard deduction); LTCG after 24 months | Months | — | Self-use; investment only with care |
| Crypto / VDA | Very low | 30% flat, 1% TDS, no loss set-off | Varies | — | Optional speculative slice ≤ 2–5% |
| Endowment / ULIP / money-back | Insurer risk; poor value | Maturity exempt only under premium caps | Very low | 5–20 yrs | Avoid new purchases |
| P2P / unlisted "assured" schemes | Low | Slab | Low | — | Avoid or cap tightly |

## Allocation guidance by goal horizon

| Horizon | Typical mix | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| < 1 year | Savings/sweep FD, liquid fund, arbitrage | Capital must be there |
| 1–3 years | FDs, T-bills/G-secs, short debt or arbitrage funds; ≤ 20% equity | Volatility can't recover in time |
| 3–7 years | 30–60% equity (index-heavy), rest high-quality debt/PPF | Balance growth and drawdown |
| 7+ years | 60–80% equity, 10–25% debt (PPF/EPF/NPS count), 5–10% gold | Growth with ballast |

Adjust for risk tolerance stated in rupees, job stability and existing concentration (e.g. RSUs already count as equity).
