# Intake

Collect everything below. Ask in one consolidated list, grouped by section; skip sections that clearly don't apply. Prefer documents — they are more accurate than recall.

## Documents to request (redacted: PAN, Aadhaar, account numbers, addresses)

| Document | What it gives you | Where the user gets it |
|---|---|---|
| Form 16 (Part A + B) | Salary, exemptions claimed, TDS, regime used by employer | Employer, after year end |
| Latest payslips (2–3) | Current CTC structure, employer NPS, reimbursements | Employer HR portal |
| AIS and TIS | Everything the department already knows: interest, dividends, securities trades, property, foreign remittances | Income-tax e-filing portal → AIS |
| Form 26AS | TDS/TCS credits, advance tax paid | Income-tax e-filing portal |
| Mutual fund CAS | All MF holdings and transactions across AMCs | CAMS / KFintech / MF Central |
| Broker capital-gains / tax P&L | Realised gains by type and holding period | Broker console |
| Foreign brokerage statements | RSU/ESPP vests, sales, dividends, year-end values, highest values | E*Trade, Schwab, Fidelity, etc. |
| Demat holdings statement | Direct equity, bonds, SGBs | NSDL/CDSL CAS |
| EPF passbook, PPF, NPS statement | Balances and contributions | EPFO, bank, CRA |
| Loan statements | Outstanding, rate, EMI, interest/principal split for the year | Lenders |
| Insurance schedules | Term cover, health cover, riders, premiums, renewal dates | Insurers / employer |
| Last year's ITR (JSON or PDF) | Carried-forward losses, regime history, form used | E-filing portal |
| Rent receipts / rental agreement | HRA claim or rental income | User |

## Questions

### Identity and residency
- Tax year in question. Age. Dependants (spouse, children, parents — and whether parents are senior citizens).
- Citizenship. Days in India this tax year and in each of the previous few years. Any plans to work abroad (residency affects what income is taxable in India).
- Any foreign income, foreign bank/brokerage account, or foreign property, ever, held at any point in the year.

### Income
- Salary: CTC breakdown (basic, HRA, special allowance, bonus, employer PF, employer NPS, ESOP/RSU).
- Stock compensation: employer, listing (Indian or foreign), vesting schedule, units vested this year, sales, current holdings.
- Rent received; property details; home loan on let-out property.
- Interest: savings, FD, bonds. Dividends.
- Capital gains realised this year by asset type; any carried-forward losses.
- Freelance, consulting, business, F&O or intraday trading (these change the ITR form and may need a CA).
- Crypto/VDA transactions.
- Contract income from abroad or a short-term overseas assignment.

### Current tax setup
- Regime chosen with the employer this year; regime used last year. Whether they have business income (switching rules differ).
- Deductions currently used: 80C items, NPS self-contribution, health premiums, home loan interest, education loan, donations.
- HRA: rent paid, to whom (parent? landlord PAN available?), city.

### Assets and liabilities
- Cash and FDs, by bank.
- Each investment: type, current value, cost, purchase date (or "multiple SIPs"), lock-in.
- Real estate: self-occupied / let-out / under construction, value, purchase date and cost.
- Gold: physical, SGB (bought at issue or secondary market), ETF/fund.
- Every loan: type, outstanding, rate, EMI, tenure left, prepayment penalty.

### Protection
- Term insurance: sum assured, insurer, whether personal or employer-only.
- Health insurance: personal policy cover and employer cover; parents' cover.
- Emergency fund: amount and where it sits.
- Nominations and a will.

### Goals and constraints
- Each goal: what, amount in today's rupees, target year, priority.
- Monthly fixed expenses and monthly investible surplus.
- Risk tolerance: what fall in portfolio value (in rupees, not %) would make them sell.
- Job stability and expected income changes (job switch, contract, sabbatical, study).
- Any ethical/religious or other constraints on instruments.

## What "enough to advise" means

Minimum before giving recommendations: residency, income by head with amounts, current regime and deductions, emergency fund, term and health cover, all debts, and at least the top goals with timelines. Everything else improves precision; flag what's missing as an open question rather than stalling.
